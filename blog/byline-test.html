Larkspur 2.4: faster exports and an audit log

By Robin Example, Software engineer · Nov 6, 2026

Larkspur 2.4 is out today. Exports of large workspaces now finish in under a minute, and every plan gets an audit log.

Faster exports

We rewrote the export pipeline to stream rows instead of building the whole file in memory. A workspace with two million rows now exports in about forty seconds.

Audit log

The audit log records sign-ins, permission changes and deleted records for ninety days. Owners can filter it by person and by date.

Also in this release

  • Saved filters now sync across devices.
  • CSV imports accept semicolons as separators.
  • Fixed: dashboard tiles no longer reload twice after sign-in.
